Use case: identity-preserve.
Asset: polished mascot software family concept sheet, refining existing selected art direction.
Input image 1 is the NEW definitive nine-character sheet: take ONLY its fourth character, 耳鼠, as identity and drawing reference. Input image 2 is the old family sheet: use only its three-function concept and left-to-right layout, update all character drawing to match image 1.
Primary request: create three beautifully matched instances of precisely the same earshu mascot holding three different functional objects. Keep the clean thin blue-gray contour, warm ivory face and chest, slate-indigo body, elongated peach inner rabbit ears, large sweeping ivory-tipped tail, tiny orange flank accent, restrained cel shading, clever gentle almond eyes and small smile. Take special care to match the ear root spacing, facial geometry, forehead cream markings, cheek contour and tail sweep across all three. Match the quieter satin surface in the new sheet rather than shiny plastic. Slight left-facing 3/4 view, same light from upper left, same scale, baseline and body silhouette.
Drawing improvement: graceful connected ear roots, intentional two forepaws with small rounded fingers, two visible hind feet, clean tangent-free tail/body connection. Functional props must be 20-25 percent of character silhouette, held slightly below chest with clear space between chin and top of prop; keep the ivory chest tuft partly visible. No disconnected paws or duplicated arms.
Left: compact cornflower-blue desktop-window card with thin indigo border, one small title dot and THREE broad ivory app panels, no tiny text. Middle: compact cream open notebook with a blue pen held by one forepaw, just two broad pale lines on each page. Right: compact warm amber folder with one legible tab and restrained corner highlight. Composition: landscape warm nearly-white board, three large characters centered on generous whitespace, no scenes, no app chrome. Top title exactly "耳鼠 · 功能家族精修". Below the characters exact labels "耳鼠·桌面", "耳鼠·笔记", "耳鼠·文件". Under a pale horizontal rule put a simple secondary row of the same three complete drawings at reduced presentation size on the same white background; do not use tile frames, grayscale conversions or invented app UI. Footer exactly "同一角色 · 功能道具示例 · 尚未定名". Crisp modest Chinese typography.
Constraints: preserve the character identity, chosen palette and selected illustration style; changing functional prop does not change facial expression, body identity or pose. This is a design study, not a photo. Avoid heavy outlines, stickers, toy rendering, extra accessories, fur texture, exaggerated giant eyes, full backgrounds, decorative badges, real software logos, fake screen-size claims.
