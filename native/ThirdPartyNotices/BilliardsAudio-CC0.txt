Preparation: selected isolated recorded impacts, removed DC, resampled to
44.1 kHz / mono / signed PCM16, faded only the cut edges, matched clip RMS
to 0.05 with a 0.90 peak ceiling, and trimmed silent lead-ins.
Cushion and pocket feedback are low-pass adaptations of pool-shot recordings;
they are not represented as separately recorded cushion/pocket sounds.
No synthesized tones are mixed into the samples. Runtime scales the amplitude
and selects soft/hard takes without changing playback speed or pitch.
Exact cut times and output SHA-256 values: Games.Core/Audio/manifest.json.
Reproduction: native/scripts/Import-BilliardsAudio.py (development only).
The recordings are packaged with the executable; no runtime download is used.
